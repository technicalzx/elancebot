A more useful review loop

From a rough offer to a more confident next draft.

Elancebot helps you organize the inputs behind a good decision. It does not access or represent any marketplace, and it cannot guarantee placement, sales, or rankings.

  1. 01

    Describe what you offer

    Add a service URL or a draft, the audience you want to serve, and the words you want to test.

  2. 02

    Add context you trust

    Paste a competitor snapshot or notes you collected yourself. We label supplied context clearly.

  3. 03

    Act on an open review

    Prioritized recommendations show their inputs and limitations, so you can decide what to change.
