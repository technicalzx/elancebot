    Your experiment plan

    Saved plans are fixed so the intended change stays clear. Create a new plan to change the hypothesis. Outcome corrections use a version check; no background saving takes place.
    

    Record outcomes
    Count unique converting opportunities, not repeated events. Use complete, equal-length date windows ending before today (UTC). Blank periods are saved as missing, never as zero. Record when you published the change and any other influences in the notes.
    Observed comparison
    

    How to interpret the outcome
    Record both the outcome count and its denominator for each complete period. Zero visits or impressions means the rate is unavailable—not zero performance. Compare equivalent traffic sources and time windows. A higher rate can be noise, particularly with few observations. Before/after data alone does not prove your change caused an improvement.
    

    What this product can and cannot do
    Elancebot supports evidence reviews, saved draft revisions, competitor snapshots, private experiment plans and manual outcome records. Automatic marketplace connections, AI rewrites and automated outcome imports remain unfinished. No ranking or revenue increase is guaranteed.
