Freelance pricing

The pricing calculator starts with your annual pre-tax income target, business costs and billable schedule. It calculates an hourly target, adds project costs, then grosses up the quote using the fee percentage you supply.

For example: 40,000 income + 10,000 annual costs, spread over 1,000 billable hours, gives 50 currency units per hour. A ten-hour project plus 100 in costs requires a gross quote of 750 when your assumed fee is 20%. This is a planning scenario, not a market-rate recommendation.

Profit margin versus markup

Profit is revenue minus cost. Margin divides profit by revenue; markup divides profit by cost. Revenue of 1,000 and costs of 600 means 400 profit, 40% margin and 66.67% markup. A zero denominator is shown as undefined instead of a misleading zero or infinity.

Salary period conversion

Convert gross hourly, weekly, monthly and annual pay using your paid hours and paid weeks. At 25 per hour, 40 hours per week and 52 paid weeks, the annual equivalent is 52,000 and the monthly average is 4,333.33. This tool does not calculate taxes, benefits or take-home pay.

Education calculations

The percentage calculator divides a part by a whole and multiplies by 100: 18 out of 24 is 75%. For GPA, supply your institution’s grade points and course credits. Three credits at 4 points and four credits at 3 points give 24 weighted points across 7 credits, or 3.429 to three decimals. Institutional repeat-course, pass/fail and rounding policies still apply.

Text and units

Count approximate words and sentences, count Unicode code points, estimate reading time at your chosen speed, and change letter case. Code points are not always the same as visible characters; language-specific segmentation may differ.

Convert length, mass and temperature using compatible units. One foot is 0.3048 metres; 32°F is 0°C. The tool rejects mixed dimensions and temperatures below absolute zero. Results display at most eight decimal places.

Profiles, gigs, services and competitor snapshots

Bring a draft and authorized supporting evidence. Reviews explain positioning, completeness, clarity, proof and discoverability inputs. Save a revision, improve the draft and compare it with the previous result. Competitor comparisons use equivalent snapshots you supply, not hidden scraping or verified market rankings.

Website metadata reviews

Approved public HTTPS websites can be checked with permission. The server bounds response size and time, does not follow redirects, and never sends your session or credentials to the website. The preview homepage uses its own rendered HTML and labels that source. Metadata checks cannot prove indexing, rankings, traffic or Core Web Vitals.

One workflow across tools

Search by category or keyword, favorite frequently used tools and return to recently opened ones. Calculations have validation, explanations and limitations. Copy or download a result, reset the form, or save the inputs and result to your private history. Account export and deletion include saved tool results and preferences.

Career, proposals and offer packages

Job evidence fit checks up to twenty requirement phrases against authorized text, shows exact excerpts and identifies phrases not found. A match is not proof of a qualification: read the surrounding context, including negation. The resume review also recognizes common English section labels. Neither tool produces a vendor ATS score.

The client brief planner organizes your objective, deliverables, constraints and evidence into an editable proposal structure with unanswered questions. Package consistency checks compare one to three entered offers in one currency and flag unclear scope or tier distinctions. No marketplace data or price recommendation is inferred.

Content outlines

Choose a post, article, email or case-study outline, record your audience and requested tone, and keep supplied facts beside the structure. This is a template with writing prompts. It does not generate prose, rewrite your text or invent an outcome. All five workflows support private saved results, reopening, copy and JSON export.
