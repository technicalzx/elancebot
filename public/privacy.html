Privacy & preview notice

Updated October 14, 2026. This public preview is experimental. Do not enter sensitive personal information, client secrets or platform credentials.

What is stored

Account name and email, a salted password verifier, hashed recovery/session/API credentials, project inputs, saved review results, optional saved calculator/utility inputs and results, favorites, recently opened tool names and timestamps. Cloudflare processes hosting and request metadata. Temporary rate-limit records contain hashed identifiers, not raw IPs or email addresses.

How it is used

To provide your private workspace, calculate requested reviews and protect the service. No advertising analytics, tracking pixels, AI model calls or third-party marketplace connections are enabled. Your content is not sent to an AI provider or sold.

URL insights and source snapshots

A saved URL review includes your own and optional competitor source text, URLs, retrieval labels, recorded times, target phrases and recommendations. Supplied snapshots are not verified live marketplace data. The optional Cloudflare Workers AI adapter is disabled. Enabling it would require an operator decision and live evaluation; each AI run would also require your consent to send the selected snapshots and context. No AI calls happen in the current preview.

Website reviews

When you request an approved URL review, the server retrieves the HTML from that website. The website operator receives the request and may log it. We do not send your password, account email, API key or browser cookies to the website.

Retention and control

Projects, reviews and saved tool results remain until you delete them or your account. Favorites and recently opened tool names persist until account deletion; favorites can also be unset. Unchecking save runs a calculation without retaining its inputs or result. Account settings provides JSON export and authenticated deletion. Deletion removes live records and revokes access; Cloudflare backups and infrastructure logs may persist for their configured retention windows. Sessions expire after seven days; API keys after 30 days. Expired credentials and rate-limit rows are cleaned on later authentication requests. This preview may be reset; keep your own exports.

Account limitations

Email ownership is not verified. There is no email delivery or MFA. Account recovery uses the one-time code provided at signup; losing both it and your password prevents self-service recovery. Scores describe supplied content, not verified achievements, platform rankings or earnings.

Cookies and local storage

An essential HttpOnly session cookie keeps you signed in. A local-storage preference remembers your selected theme. No optional tracking cookies are used.

Operator and production readiness

This preview is maintained through the ycgiio/elancebot repository. Contact the operator through their verified GitHub profile; never post private data in public issues. Before a production launch, the operator must publish an appropriate contact channel, retention schedule, lawful basis, applicable terms and processor agreements. This notice is not a claim of GDPR, SOC 2, ISO 27001 or other certification.
